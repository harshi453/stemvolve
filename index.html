About STEMvolve

STEMvolve was founded by a 14-year-old high school freshman who realized that figuring out what career you want to pursue is one of the hardest things about being a student — and nobody was making it easier.

Most career websites are built by adults who don't remember what it's like to be in middle or high school, overwhelmed with choices and unsure where to start.

So we built STEMvolve — a platform where students can explore real careers, watch interviews with real professionals, and start planning their future while they still have time to act on it.

Built by students. For students. Always free.

🎯

Our Mission

To help every middle and high school student discover their dream career path before they graduate — giving them the clarity, confidence, and roadmap to make it happen.
